🏰 Guidelines & Battle Rules

  • Gameplay: Teams take turns completing the imperative prompt.
  • LP Conversion: HP lost will be converted into LP lost divided by 2.
  • Typing Rules: Capitalization, extra spaces, and basic punctuation (like ! or .) are ignored. Exact spelling is required!
  • Wrong Answer: Penalizes the active team by -1 HP.
  • Correct Answer: Gives your team a choice:
    • 🛠️ Repair: Restore +1 HP (Uncapped).
    • ⚔️ Attack: Deduct -2 HP from any rival team.
  • Clicking "Next Question" automatically rotates turns to the next team. You can also click any team's card at the top to set their turn manually.
