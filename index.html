CLASS QUEST JOURNAL: LP & GOLD GUIDE

Welcome to Class Quest Journal! Your active participation, English discipline, and teamwork are directly tied to your Life Points (LP) and Stardust Gold (GP).

🛡️ 1. The 50 LP Grade Scale

Every cadet begins each cycle with 50 LP (full vitality bar).

LP Range Grade Equivalent Status Classification
45 – 50 LP A+ (90 – 100%) Vanguard Hero
40 – 44 LP A (80 – 89%) Veteran Scholar
31 – 39 LP B (70 – 79%) Adventurer
26 – 30 LP C (60 – 69%) On Notice
Below 25 LP D / Re-evaluation Danger Zone (Teacher Intervention)

⚡ 2. English Hour Conduct Directives

  • Violating English Hour (-1 LP): Speaking native language without permit during designated immersion phases.
  • Non-English Permit (-1 LP): Requesting a pass to explain complex queries in native language.
  • AWOL (-3 LP): Leaving class or unexcused inactivity during mission tasks.
  • Nonparticipation (-1 LP): Reluctance to engage in dialogues or group quests.
