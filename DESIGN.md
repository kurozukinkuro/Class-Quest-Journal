## Brand & Style

This design system channels a clean, luminous fantasy anime aesthetic centered on celestial wonder, pristine clarity, and high-spec speculative interfaces. It draws directly from the refined visual language of modern anime role-playing games, prestige gacha HUDs, and ethereal skyward fantasy. 

The aesthetic unites three distinct stylistic pillars:
- **Ethereal High-Key Minimalism:** Pristine pearl surfaces, vast breathing room, and soft starlit skies eliminate the clutter of traditional fantasy interfaces in favor of pure, modern elegance.
- **Glassmorphism & Luminous Depth:** Translucent crystalline cards, refracted ambient rim lighting, and starlit glow halos provide a floating, weightless spatial quality.
- **Precision HUD Geometry:** Constellation-inspired diamond glyphs, sharp astronomical markers, and calibrated micro-accents contrast against soft organic silhouettes to invoke an arcane yet technologically advanced sky-realm.

The visual experience evokes awe, precision, and optimistic wonder—inviting users into an immaculately organized, elevated world where every interactive element feels like interacting with a relic carved of crystallized starlight.

## Colors

The palette operates in a pristine, high-luminance light mode designed to capture the dawn sky above the cloud layer. 

- **Primary (`#6366f1` Astral Indigo):** The core active accent, conveying arcane resonance, focus states, primary buttons, and key interactive conduits.
- **Secondary (`#0ea5e9` Celestial Azure):** Represents crystal energy, mana reservoirs, sub-actions, active telemetry, and ethereal cyan ambient highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#f59e0b` Starlight Gold):** Reserved for prestige status, legendary tiers, star ratings, critical notifications, and high-tier achievements.
- **Accent Coral (`#f43f5e` Blossom Rose):** An emotive contrast hue used for critical state flags, favorite actions, vital stats, and radiant blossom alerts.
- **Neutral Core (`#0f172e` Midnight Navy):** Replaces pure black with a rich, ink-like abyssal navy to deliver razor-sharp typographic contrast without harshness.
- **Canvas & Surface Tiering:**
  - Base Sky Canvas: Subtle top-to-bottom gradient shifting from `#f3f4fd` (lavender-tinged dawn) to `#f7f9ff` (pure atmospheric horizon).
  - Surface Glass: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)` layered with fine frosted micro-grain.
  - Surface Pure: `#ffffff` for elevated interactive modals and active card selections.
  - Border Sheen: `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.12)` transitioning to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)` on top-facing edges to mimic directional ambient starlight.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes bold celestial geometric display type against approachable humanist sans-serif body text, anchored by monospaced HUD telemetries.

- **Display & Headlines (`Sora`):** Chosen for its wide, geometric curves and crisp mechanical balance. In title applications, Sora delivers an assertive, futuristic fantasy presence reminiscent of title cards in modern anime epics.
- **Body & Editorial (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Soft, open, and hyper-legible. It prevents cognitive strain in descriptive lore panels, item descriptions, and user dialogues while harmonizing with Sora's round apertures.
- **HUD & Status Labels (`JetBrains Mono`):** Applied in uppercase to all numeric readouts, system telemetry, tier classifications, quest timers, and technical coordinates to evoke a calibrated astral device.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout structure relies on a floating, responsive 12-column fluid grid system engineered around widescreen anime game HUD proportions while remaining fully adaptive to handheld devices.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid, `margin-lg` (3rem) outer margins, and `gutter-lg` (2rem) column gutters. Wide viewports support floating HUD anchor trays on the left and right peripheries, framing a centered primary stage.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid, `margin` (1.5rem), and `gutter` (1.5rem). Lateral trays collapse into bottom or drawer docks.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `margin-sm` (1rem), and `gutter-sm` (1rem). Floating cards stack into cohesive vertical streams with edge-to-edge touch targets.

Visual cadence relies on generous negative space around high-density status clusters: micro HUD elements cluster tightly using `space-xs` and `space-sm`, while content cards and surface panels separate broadly using `space-lg` and `space-xl` to preserve an airy, skyward atmosphere.

## Elevation & Depth

Elevation is rendered not through murky darkness, but through layered refraction, luminous halos, and crisp celestial backlights.

- **Level 0 (Atmospheric Canvas):** The base background featuring an ethereal wash (`#f3f4fd` to `#f7f9ff`) with optional subtle celestial starfield particles rendered at 20% opacity.
- **Level 1 (Docked Panels & Sub-layers):** Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`, backdrop blur 16px, bound by a 1px solid rim of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`. Soft diffuse shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Cards):** Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`, backdrop blur 24px, bordered by `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.12)`. Shadow: dual drop of `0 4px 12px rgba(15, 23, 46, 0.03)` and `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.10)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, & Radiant Focus):** Background `#ffffff`, bordered by `rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.25)`. Shadow: pronounced astral bloom `0 0 0 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9) inset`, `0 20px 48px -10px rgba(15, 23, 46, 0.08)`, and an ambient outer glow of `0 0 32px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.15)`.
- **Luminous Edge Rule:** Every floating surface utilizes a top-edge pseudo-border highlight (`1px linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, rgba(255, 255, 255, 1), transparent)`) simulating zenith starlight catching a beveled glass edge.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined `rounded-2` baseline, which balances sleek, modern soft-geometry with the ergonomic curves of premium tech devices.

- **Base Components:** Standard buttons, chips, text fields, and list entries use `rounded` (0.5rem / 8px) to `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px).
- **Cards & Surfaces:** Container panels, quest scrolls, and modal frames use `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) for a gentle, pillowed anime-glass aesthetic.
- **Diamond Badges & Starlight Nodes:** Micro badges, tier tags, and level indicators reject standard rounded rectangles in favor of 4-point star silhouettes or 45-degree rotated diamond polygons (`polygon(50% 0%, 100% 50%, 50% 100%, 0% 50%)`).
- **Corner Accents:** Selected prestige headers and quest trackers incorporate subtle 45-degree diagonal notched corners (chamfers) paired with glowing corner ticks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Celestial Action:** Gradient fill from Indigo (`#6366f1`) to Sky Azure (`#0ea5e9`), text `#ffffff` in `Sora` semi-bold. Shadow: `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.35)`. Subtle four-point diamond star icon precedes or follows the label. Hover triggers a radiant brightness boost (`brightness(1.08)`) and an outward cyan ring pulse.
- **Secondary Ghost Crystal:** Surface `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`, border `1px solid rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.2)`, text `#0f172e`. Hover applies `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.05)` fill and crisp indigo border.
- **Tertiary Starlight:** Background `linear-gradient(135deg, #f59e0b, #fbbf24)`, text `#0f172e` bold, used exclusively for primary claim actions, summon triggers, and gacha pulls.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact pill silhouettes (`rounded-full`) or faceted diamond badges.
- Tinted semi-transparent backdrops (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.08)` for astral, `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)` for legendary).
- Text styled strictly in `label-sm` or `label-md` uppercase JetBrains Mono, accompanied by a miniature luminous dot or star glyph.

### Cards & Panels
- Constructed using frosted glass surfaces (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`).
- Rimmed with a soft two-tier border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)` on top and sides, softly fading into `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.1)` along the base.
- Top right of important cards carries an ethereal HUD accent mark: double chevron, celestial coordinate, or glowing tier badge.

### Inputs & Selectors
- Background `#ffffff` with an inner border of `rgba(15, 23, 46, 0.08)`. Height is standardized to 44px with a 12px border radius.
- Inactive placeholder styled in `Plus Jakarta Sans`, color `#94a3b8`.
- Focus state casts an instant double aura: `0 0 0 2px #ffffff`, `0 0 0 4px #0ea5e9`, and an ambient azure shadow bloom.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Checkboxes: 20px rounded squares (6px corner radius) with pearl-white background and indigo borders. Checked state fills with `#6366f1` and reveals a clean white diamond or check glyph.
- Radio buttons: Outer concentric ring with an inner glowing circular core that scales up smoothly with spring physics on selection.

### Custom HUD Elements: Quest Banner & Mana Gauge
- **Quest Tracker Ribbon:** Asymmetric floating bar with a left-aligned diamond milestone indicator and an active progress track glowing in `#0ea5e9`.
- **Celestial Status Meter:** Segmented horizontal progress bars backed by translucent frosted tracks, where active progress radiates a linear gradient with a shimmering white light sweep animation running across its edge.
